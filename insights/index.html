FAMILY LAW / GUIDE

Preparing for a Divorce Consultation

What to gather before your first discussion with a family-law attorney.

Preparing documents and questions can help you make the most of a legal consultation.

Gather your marriage details, relevant financial records, information about children where applicable and a timeline of key events. Avoid sharing sensitive details through public forms.

Speak to a legal practitioner about your specific facts and available legal options.

Note: This information is general and does not constitute legal advice.

DEBT REVIEW / GUIDE

Debt Review: Understanding Your Options

A starting point for people facing debt-related disputes.

Debt processes can be difficult to navigate without a clear picture of what has happened.

Keep your debt-review documents, statements, notices and communications together. Different forms of relief or dispute resolution may depend on the particular circumstances.

Do not assume that removing a debt-review listing is automatically possible; obtain individual legal advice.

Note: This information is general and does not constitute legal advice.

ESTATES / GUIDE

Deceased Estates: Where to Begin

General considerations when a family member dies.

Estate administration involves documents, beneficiaries and statutory processes.

Useful initial documents may include a death certificate, will (if one exists), identity documents, details of assets and liabilities and the particulars of family members.

Consult a South African estates practitioner to clarify obligations and timelines.

Note: This information is general and does not constitute legal advice.
